# AESencryption
Realisation of AES encryption


## Сборка

1. Установи MSYS2 и GMP:
   pacman -S mingw-w64-x86_64-gmp

2. Скачай httplib.h:
   curl -o third_party/httplib/httplib.h https://raw.githubusercontent.com/yhirose/cpp-httplib/master/httplib.h

3. Собери проект:
   mingw32-make
